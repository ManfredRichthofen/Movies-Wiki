The **globe** icon next to Search turns **extra sources** on or off. When it is on, the player may look for more places to play a title. That can help find some content, but it also uses more data and can make search and playback feel slower on a weak connection.

:::warning[Slow connection?]
If pages take forever to load, searches hang, or video buffers more than usual, **turn extra sources off**. Your regular library still works without them.
:::

## Turn extra sources off

1. Open [jfapp.xyz](https://jfapp.xyz) and sign in.
2. Go to the **Search** bar at the top.
3. Tap the **globe** icon to the left of Search to turn extra sources off.

When they are off, the icon becomes a **globe with a slash**:

![Extra sources off — globe icon with a slash next to the search bar](/img/remote-stream-disabled.png)

Tap the same icon again to turn them back on (for example after you switch to faster Wi‑Fi).

## When to leave them on

Keep extra sources on when your connection is stable and you want the player to check more places for a title. Prefer leaving them off on mobile data, busy Wi‑Fi, or any network that already feels slow.

## Still having trouble?

- Start with the [troubleshooting guide](/docs/jellyfin/troubleshooting/) for buffering, login, and connection fixes.
- Lower stream quality in the player settings (gear icon during playback).
- See [Watch on any device](/docs/jellyfin/watch-anywhere/) for device-specific fixes.
- If a title is missing from the library, [ask for it](/docs/jellyfin/request-titles/) instead of relying on extra sources.
