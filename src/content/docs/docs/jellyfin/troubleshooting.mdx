Use this page when streaming feels slow, search hangs, or you can’t sign in. Work top to bottom — most people fix it in the first two sections.

:::tip[Quick checks]
- Server address is always **`jfapp.xyz`** (most apps: no `https://`)
- Check [status.jfapp.xyz](https://status.jfapp.xyz) if nothing loads
- Try Wi‑Fi if mobile data fails (or the other way around)
:::

## Buffering or slow playback

1. **Lower the quality** — while a video is playing, open the gear icon and pick a lower resolution.
2. **Turn off extra sources** — the globe icon next to Search can slow things down on a weak connection. See the next section.
3. **Move closer to Wi‑Fi** or use a wired connection if you can.
4. On a phone or TV app, try pausing for a few seconds, then play again.

Still buffering after that? See device-specific tips in [Watch on any device](/docs/jellyfin/watch-anywhere/).

## Extra sources (globe icon)

The **globe** next to Search turns extra playback sources on or off. When it’s on, the player may look for more places to play a title. That helps sometimes, but it uses more data and can make **search and playback feel slow**.

**If pages load slowly, searches hang, or video buffers more than usual — turn it off:**

1. Open [jfapp.xyz](https://jfapp.xyz) and sign in.
2. Go to the **Search** bar.
3. Tap the **globe** to the left of Search until you see a **globe with a slash**.

![Extra sources off — globe with a slash next to Search](/img/remote-stream-disabled.png)

Your regular library still works with extra sources off. Full walkthrough: [Extra sources (globe icon)](/docs/jellyfin/remote-stream/).

## Can’t connect or sign in

### “Unable to connect to server” / server not found

- Enter exactly `jfapp.xyz` (no `https://` in most mobile/TV apps; desktop may use `https://jfapp.xyz`).
- Turn off VPN temporarily and try again.
- Check [status.jfapp.xyz](https://status.jfapp.xyz).
- Remove the saved server in the app and add `jfapp.xyz` again.

### “Invalid username or password”

- Check caps lock and spelling.
- Make sure you finished your [invite link setup](/docs/jellyfin/set-up-account/) first.
- Wait a minute after setup, then try again.
- Ask the admin for a password reset if you’re locked out.

### Works in the browser but not in the app

- Update the app from the store or the [Downloads](/Downloads/) page.
- Delete the server entry and add `jfapp.xyz` again.
- Confirm the same username and password work at [jfapp.xyz](https://jfapp.xyz).

## Picture or sound problems

| What you see | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Video but no sound | Turn **Direct play** off in the app settings, or lower quality |
| Green or purple colors (desktop) | Client Settings → Video → set **Hardware decoding** to **Disabled** |
| Audio out of sync | Turn hardware decoding off; update the app and graphics drivers |
| 4K stutters on TV | Drop quality to 1080p |

More detail by device: [Watch on any device](/docs/jellyfin/watch-anywhere/).
